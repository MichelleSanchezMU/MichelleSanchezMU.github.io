About

I’m a psychology major. Most of my days are spent reading about how people think — which turns out to be lovely company for a craft that asks you to stop thinking for a while.

I learned pottery in an elective art course, expecting to take one class and move on. Instead I found something I looked forward to all week.

Everything here is made purely for fun. Nothing is for sale and nothing is on a deadline. These are just the pieces I liked enough to keep and images of some of the process.
